## 四、17 章逐章精要

### 第 1 章 名词（p1-16）
- 分类：专有名词（大写、一般不加 the；例外 the UK/the UN/the Smiths 一家人）；普通名词=个体/集体/物质/抽象
- 可数复数规则：+s；s/x/ch/sh 结尾 +es；辅音+y→ies（元音+y 只 +s：day/toy/boy/key）；f/fe→ves（knife/wife/leaf/shelf 等）；不规则 child→children、foot→feet、tooth→teeth、mouse→mice、man→men、woman→women；单复同形 sheep/deer/fish/Chinese/Japanese；只有复数 trousers/clothes/glasses/scissors（须 a pair of）
- 不可数量化：a piece of news/paper/advice、a bottle/cup/glass of…；复数落在量词上 two pieces of news
- 所有格：单数 +'s；s 结尾复数 +'；不规则复数 children's；**共有 vs 各有：Mary and Jane's mother（同母）/ Mary's and Jane's mothers（各自）**；无生命用 of（the door of the room）；双重所有格 a friend of my father's；时间/距离/国家可用 's（today's newspaper、ten minutes' walk、China's population）
- 主谓一致：语法一致（every/each of/either of/many a +单数；news/maths/physics 形复意单）；就近一致（there be、either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also）；意义一致（family/class/team 看语境；时间金钱距离复数形式单数概念；people/police 恒复数）

### 第 2 章 代词（p17-25）
- 人称代词主格/宾格全表；多个人称排序（单数 you, he and I；复数 we, you and they）；it 指天气/时间/距离/不明性别婴儿
- 物主代词：形容词性 vs 名词性（mine/yours/his/hers/its/ours/theirs），名词性 = 形容词性+名词
- 反身代词全表；固定搭配 enjoy oneself、help oneself to、teach oneself = learn…by oneself、dress oneself
- 指示：this/these 近、that/those 远；**that/those 替代前文名词避免重复（The weather in Beijing is colder than that in Shanghai.）**
- 不定代词：some 肯定/表请求建议的疑问句，any 否定/疑问；复合不定代词 +形容词后置（something interesting），作主语谓语单数
- few/a few（可数）与 little/a little（不可数）：带 a 肯定、不带 a 否定
- all/both（都）+复数；either（两者任一）+单数；neither（两者都不）+单数；none（≥3 都不）；no one 不接 of
- one（同名异物）vs it（同名同物）；other/others/the other/the others/another 辨析；each（≥2，可 each of）vs every（≥3，只接单数）；each other
- 疑问代词 what/who/whom/whose/which

### 第 3 章 数词（p26-31）
- 基数拼写陷阱：thirteen/fifteen/eighteen、twenty/thirty/forty（无 u）/fifty/eighty/ninety；几十几加连字符；百位后 and
- 序数：first/second/third 特殊；fifth/eighth/ninth（去 e）/twelfth；整十 y→ieth；几十几只变个位；**序数词前通常加 the（手写强调点）**
- 分数：分子基数、分母序数，分子>1 分母 +s（two thirds）；1/2 = a half，1/4 = a quarter
- 算式 plus/minus/times/divided by；编号"名词+基数词、首字母大写"（Class 2, Grade 6；Lesson One = the first lesson）
- 时间：顺读 six ten；逆读 ≤30 分用 past（ten past six）、>30 分用 to（five to twelve）、15 分 a quarter、30 分 half；年份 in 1998 = nineteen ninety-eight；年龄 at the age of twelve = when he was twelve

### 第 4 章 冠词（p32-36）★默写重灾区
- the：特指/二次提及/独一无二（the sun/moon/earth）/序数词最高级/乐器（play the piano）/江河湖海山脉（the Yellow River、the Alps）/姓氏复数（the Smiths）/**the+adj 表一类人，谓语复数（the old）**
- the 固定搭配（课外班默写范围）：take the place of、at the end of、at the moment、with the help of、by the way、in the end、on the other hand、for the first time、for the time being、in the future
- a/an：按**读音**不按字母——an hour、an honest boy、a university、a European country；表"每一" five days a week
- a 固定搭配：take a look、have a rest、have a good time、go on a diet、have a cold/fever/headache、have a talk/walk/swim、a few/a little/a lot of
- 零冠词：三餐（at breakfast、have lunch）、球类（play basketball）、学科语言、节假日、by+交通（by bus）、in danger、in hospital、on foot、from door to door、day and night

### 第 5 章 形容词（p37-38）
- 作定语前置；**修饰复合不定代词后置（something important）**；只作表语的形容词 afraid/alone/asleep/awake/ill；the+adj 类指；多形容词排序（限定-描绘-大小-形状-年龄-颜色-国籍-材料-用途）

### 第 6 章 副词（p39-43）★课外班当前进度
- 分类：方式/时间/频率（always>usually>often>sometimes>seldom>never）/地点/程度（very/quite/too/so/**enough 后置**：good enough，但 enough time 前置）
- 比较级构成：+er/est、双写（big/hot）、辅 y→i、多音节 more/most；不规则 good/well→better→best、bad/badly/ill→worse→worst、many/much→more→most、little→less→least、far→farther/further、old→older/elder
- 句型：as…as / not as(so)…as；than；**much/a little/even/far +比较级**；比较级+and+比较级（越来越）；**the+比较级, the+比较级（越…越…，手写例句 The less you eat, the thinner you will be.）**；Which is +比较级, A or B?；the+最高级+in/of；one of the+最高级+复数名词；**比较对象一致用 that/those（手写 The weather in Shanghai is wetter than that in Beijing.）**
- 形副同词陷阱：hard（努力）/hardly（几乎不）、late/lately、near/nearly、high/highly

### 第 7 章 介词（p44-58）★固定搭配宝库
- 时间三分：at+时刻、on+天/日期、in+年/月/季节/早午晚；during vs in；since+时点、for+时段；not…until；by+时间（不迟于）
- 位置：in（大）/at（点）/on（面）；above/over 与 below/under；between/among；**across（表面穿）/through（内部穿）/over（越过）**；in front of vs in the front of；into/out of；onto/off
- 动词+介词：listen to、look at/for/after、wait for、ask for、arrive at/in、get to、come from、hear from、laugh at、talk about/to、think about/of、worry about、work on
- be+形容词+介词：be good at/for/to、be interested in、be afraid of、be angry with、be busy with、be different from、be famous for/as、be fond of、be full of、be late for、be pleased with、be proud of、be ready for、be strict with/in、be worried about

### 第 8 章 连词（p59-64）
- 并列：and/or（否定句的"和"；表"否则"）/but/for/so；关联：both…and（复数谓语）、not only…but also（就近）、either…or（就近）、neither…nor（就近）
- 从属：时间 when/while/as/before/after/until/since/as soon as；原因 because/since/as（**because 不与 so 同句**）；目的 so that；结果 so…that/such…that；条件 if/unless；让步 though/although（**不与 but 同句**）

### 第 9 章 动词（p65-81）★核心弱区对应章
- 分类：及物/不及物；连系动词 be、look/sound/smell/taste/feel、get/become/turn/grow、keep/stay/seem（后接形容词）
- 助动词：do/does/did、have/has/had、be、will/would
- **时态九宫（构成+信号词，做软件的题干生成直接取此表）**：
  - 一般现在：V 原/三单（+s、+es、y→ies；have→has）｜always/usually/often/sometimes/every day/on Sundays｜经常性、真理、时刻表
  - 一般过去：V-ed（+ed/+d/双写/y→ied）或不规则（do-did、go-went、see-saw、eat-ate、take-took、write-wrote、is/am-was、are-were、have-had、buy-bought、bring-brought、catch-caught、come-came、cut-cut、drink-drank、drive-drove、fall-fell、feel-felt、find-found、fly-flew、forget-forgot、get-got、give-gave、grow-grew、hear-heard、hide-hid、hurt-hurt、keep-kept、know-knew、leave-left、lie-lay、lose-lost、make-made、meet-met、put-put、read-read、ride-rode、ring-rang、run-ran、say-said、sell-sold、send-sent、sing-sang、sit-sat、sleep-slept、speak-spoke、stand-stood、swim-swam、teach-taught、tell-told、think-thought、throw-threw、understand-understood、wear-wore、win-won）｜yesterday/last…/…ago/just now/in 1998｜**did/didn't+原形（三单还原，高频易错）**
  - 现在进行：be+doing｜now/Look!/Listen!/at the moment｜come/go/leave/start 可表将来
  - 过去进行：was/were+doing｜at that time/this time yesterday/when+一般过去
  - 一般将来：will/shall+原形；be going to（计划/迹象）｜tomorrow/next…/soon/in+时段｜won't
  - 过去将来：would+原形（宾从/间接引语）
  - 现在完成：have/has+pp｜already/just/yet/ever/never/since/for｜**延续性转换 buy→have had、borrow→have kept、die→have been dead、leave→have been away、join→have been in**｜have been to（去过已回）vs have gone to（去了未回）
  - 过去完成：had+pp｜by the time/before+过去
  - 现在完成进行：have/has been+doing
- There be：就近原则；与 have（所属）区别；there will be / there must be
- **被动语态（p75-77，《被动语态今日营》底本）**：be+pp；各时态构成 am/is/are done、was/were done、will be done、have/has been done、can/must/should be done；主动→被动三步（宾变主、动变 be done、主加 by 可省）；不及物动词无被动
- 情态动词：can/could（能力/请求；can't 表"不可能"）；may/might；**must（必须；Must…? 否定答 needn't；mustn't=禁止）vs have to（客观不得不）**；need（need do / need to do）；shall（Shall we 建议）；will/would（Would you please）；should/ought to；used to（过去常常）

### 第 10 章 不定式（p82-85）
- 作主语/It is+adj+for(of) sb to do；作宾语 want/wish/hope/decide/agree/learn/plan/promise+to do；作宾补 tell/ask/want/would like/teach/allow sb to do，但 **make/let/have sb do（不带 to）**、see/hear/watch sb do/doing；否定 not to do；作状语（目的、too…to、enough to）；作定语（something to eat）；疑问词+to do（how to do it / what to do）

### 第 11 章 动名词（p86-88）
- 作主语（Swimming is good exercise.）；作宾语 **enjoy/finish/practice/keep/mind/suggest+doing**；介词后（be good at doing、look forward to doing、Thank you for doing）；It is no use doing、spend time doing

### 第 12 章 分词（p89-90）
- -ing（主动/进行/令人…）vs -ed（被动/完成/感到…）：interesting/interested、exciting/excited；作定语/表语/宾补（keep the door closed）

### 第 13 章 基本句型（p91-99）
- 陈述/疑问（一般、特殊、选择、反意）/祈使（原形开头，否定 Don't）/感叹（**What+(a/an)+adj+n！vs How+adj/adv！**）
- 简单/并列/复合句；五种基本句型 SV、SV+状、SVO、SVOO（双宾 give sb sth）、SVOC；倒装 here/there 开头、so/neither/nor+助动词

### 第 14 章 宾语从句（p100-103）
- 连词 that（可省）/if/whether（是否）/wh- 词；**陈述语序**；时态呼应（主现从任意、主过从过、真理永真一般现在）；否定前移（I don't think…）；if"是否"（宾从）vs"如果"（状从）
- **wish 后虚拟（p103）**：与现在相反→过去式/were（I wish it were true.）；与过去相反→had+pp；与将来相反→should/would/could/might+原形（I wish it would stop raining soon.）
- **suggest/order 类动词后宾从虚拟（p103）**：从句用 (should)+原形，**that 不可省略**（She suggested that we (should) have lunch at the new restaurant.）；同类动词 ask/demand/desire/require/urge/insist/maintain/request/recommend/advise/suggest/propose/prefer/arrange/order/direct/command/decide/determine

### 第 15 章 状语和状语从句（p104-111）
- 功能位置：副词/介词短语/不定式/分词均可作状语；**多状语顺序：方式→地点→时间**（He did his experiment carefully in the laboratory yesterday.）
- 时间（when/as/while/before/after/until/till/since/as soon as/by the time）：while 表两件事同时、as 表变化进展；**主将从现：主句一般将来，时间状从用一般现在**（As soon as I finish the job I'll give you a call.）；分词作时间状语（While waiting for the train…）
- 地点（where）；方式（as；**as if/as though 常用虚拟**：She spoke to me as if she had known me.）
- 目的（so that/in order that，可与 so as to/in order to 转换）
- 结果（so…that/such…that/so that，可与 too…to/enough to 转换；**such a cold night = so cold a night**）
- 原因（because/as/since/now that；**because 语势最强、as 次之、since 最弱；because 与 so 不同句**——手写强调）
- 条件（if/unless"除非"）：**主将从现同样适用**（We won't take action unless everyone agrees.）；**if 虚拟三式**——与现在相反 If+过去式/were…would+原形（If I were you…）；与过去相反 If+had+pp…would have+pp；与将来相反 If+过去式/were to+原形 或 should+原形表"万一"（If it should rain tomorrow, don't expect me.）
- 让步（though/although/even if/even though/no matter what/how/when = whatever/however/whenever）：**不与 but 同句**；in spite of+名词词组（手写"转换句"）；**even if/even though 常用虚拟**（Even if we failed a hundred times, we wouldn't lose heart.）
- 比较（than/as：The maglev goes much faster than the light rail train does. = …doesn't go as(so)fast as…）
- 伴随（分词：The teacher sat near the stove, holding a book in his hand.）

### 第 16 章 定语和定语从句（p112-114）
- 定语：前置（形容词/名词/数词/分词）与后置（介词短语/不定式/分词/副词：nothing wrong with the machine、the man standing at the entrance、the fish caught yesterday）
- 关系代词：**who/whom 指人、which 指物、that 人物皆可、whose 表所属人/物皆可**（old buildings whose windows were all broken = the windows of which…）
- **优先用 that**：先行词被最高级/序数词/the only/the same/the very/all/every/any/no 修饰时；先行词为人时通常 who（anyone who knows Spanish）
- 关系代词作宾语可省略（the man (whom/that) I met yesterday）
- **主谓一致：从句谓语随先行词单复数**；**one of+复数名词+定从→谓语复数（one of the students who know…）；the only one of+复数名词+定从→谓语单数（the only one of the students who knows…）**
- 关系副词：when（=at which）、where（=in which）、why（=for which，the reason why）

### 第 17 章 语言功能（p115-117）
- Thank you/Thanks 应答 16 组：How are you/Good luck/Have a good time（The same to you）/Congratulations/Happy birthday/You speak English very fluently/Help yourself to some dumplings（No, thanks. I've had enough.）等
- would like 引导及应答：Would you like a drink?（Yes, please./No, thanks.）；Would you like to join us?（**I'd love to, but… Thank you all the same.**）；Would you like me to book a ticket?（No, you needn't. Thank you all the same.）
- Would…mind 与 Sorry：Would you mind turning on the fan?（All right./I'll do it right now.）；Would you mind my opening the window?（**Not at all./Of course not./Certainly not.**）；I'm sorry.（That's all right./Never mind./Don't worry.）
- 对建议的应答：I think you should…/You'd better not…/Shall we…?/Let's…（Yes, let's./I'm afraid it's too late.）/What(How) about…?（That's a good idea./That sounds interesting./Sure, why not?）
