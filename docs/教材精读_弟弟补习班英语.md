## 二、逐课精要（L1–L23）

**L1 名词概念 & 可数/不可数**：noun = naming word（place/person/animal/thing）；可数可数数、可加 s；不可数（bread/juice/milk/rice）不加 a/an、无复数。

**L2 名词单复数**：+s（table/pear/visitor/radio）；s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾 +es（dress/fox/beach/dish/potato/tomato）；辅音+y→ies（city/family/baby/butterfly）；元音+y 只 +s（day/toy/boy/key）。

**L3 名词所有格 & To be**：单数 +'s（Tom's football）；s 结尾复数 +'（dogs' ball、grandparents' house）；Be 家族肯定/否定全表（am/is/are + not，缩写 'm not/isn't/aren't）。

**L4 情态动词 & 行为动词**：action verbs（run/play/swim/draw/eat/cry）；can 表能力，cannot = can't（Robin can't eat food.）；should 表建议，shouldn't（She shouldn't draw pictures on the desks.）。

**L5 形容词**：describe nouns；分类 Color/Size/Shape/Feel/Feature；两个位置：名词前（colorful feathers）、be 后（A dog is clever.）。

**L6 副词**：修饰动词表 How（quickly/carefully/patiently/slowly）；频率条 always 100→usually 90→often 70→sometimes 50→never 0；When（now/yesterday/tomorrow/soon）；Where（here/there/inside/outside/nearby）；构成 +ly（quick/slow/strong）、y→ily（happy/heavy/easy）；**陷阱：friendly/lovely/lonely/ugly 是形容词**；同形 fast/late；good→well 特殊。

**L7 人称代词**：主格/宾格全表（I-me、we-us、you-you、he-him、she-her、it-it、they-them）；宾格用于动词后（Jim sees me.）和介词后（for me、to him）。

**L8 物主代词**：形容词性 my/our/your/his/her/its/their vs 名词性 mine/ours/yours/his/hers/its/theirs；**possessive pronoun = possessive adjective + noun（mine = my book）**。

**L9 时间/地点介词**：Place（in/on/at/under/behind/near/in front of/next to = beside/between）；Movement（to/from/into/up/down/onto）；Manner（by/with/on/through/in）；Time 三分：**in+月/季/年/the morning（in December、in winter、in 2020）、on+星期/节日/日期（on Monday、on Tree-planting Day、on August 1st）、at+时刻/noon/night/midnight（at 8:00）**；before/after、from…to…。

**L10 不定冠词**：a/an 按读音（a university、a European country；an hour、an honest boy）；三种语义：= one（I have a dog.）、= any（A horse is a useful animal.）、= every（five days a week、100 km an hour）；**必须用 a：have a walk/fever/cold/rest/good time；不能用 a：have breakfast/lunch/dinner；by bus/train/plane/bike/underground 与 on foot 不加冠词**。

**L11 数词与时间表达**：基数 1-100 拼写（eleven/twelve/thirteen/fifteen/twenty/thirty/forty/fifty/eighty/ninety）；几十几 = 几十-几（twenty-one、fifty-four、seventy-seven）；时间：整点 o'clock；顺读（6:10 six ten、11:45 eleven forty-five）；逆读 minute≤30 → minute+past+hour（ten past six）；minute>30 → (60-minute)+to+(hour+1)（five to twelve、twenty to nine）。

**L13 There be**：表存在；There is+a/an/one+单数；There is+some/much+不可数；There are+数词/some/many+复数；**就近原则**（There is an apple and two peaches. / There are two peaches and an apple.）；否定用 not+any（There isn't any…）；缩写 There's/isn't/aren't。

**L14 句子类型(1)**：陈述句（肯定/否定）；否定构成：be+not；can/should+not；非三单 do not+原形；**三单 does not+原形**；疑问句：Yes/No 问句（Are you…? Can he…? Do you…?）+ 短答；Wh- 问句开头（Who is she? What do you like to eat?）。

**L15 句子类型(2)**：一般疑问句转换总表（Be+主语? / Can·Should+主语? / Do+非三单? / **Does+三单+原形**?）；祈使句（Turn off the TV! Listen to me!；please 可前可后；否定 Don't+原形）；感叹句（**What+(a/an)+adj+noun!**：What a cute dog! / What nice weather!；**How+adj!**：How happy!；表喜怒惊讶）。

**L16 特殊疑问句 who/what**：wh-question 答具体信息；question words 树（who/what/when/where/which/whose/why/how）；who 问人（Lisa meets Anna. → Who does Lisa meet?）；what 问物（Jim likes books. → What does Jim like?）；what…do 问动作；What+noun（What color/time/day…?）。

**L17 which/whose**：which+noun 表有限选择（Which color do you like, red or yellow?）；which vs what（已知范围 vs 未知）；whose 问所属，造句三步（It is my hat. → Is it my hat? → Whose hat is this?）；回答用物主形容词/所有格（my room、Linda's、my parents'）。

**L18 when/where**：when 问时间/日期（When do you usually go jogging? In the morning. / When is your birthday? On May 8th. / When were you born? On June 12th, 2005.）；when vs what time（具体时刻）；where 问地点（Where are the books?）；wh- 造句法：陈述→一般疑问→替换疑问词。

**L19 why/how**：why 问原因、because 答（Why are you late for work? Because I got up late.）；how 问感受（How do you feel? happy/sad/angry/hungry/tired）、问特征（How is the house? big/small/tall）、**问交通方式（by plane/bus/car/bicycle/train/underground；on foot）**。

**L20 现在进行时**：Function = happening now；Form = Subject+be(am/is/are)+doing；信号词 now/Look!/Listen!/Please be quiet!；-ing 构成三规则：+ing（sleep/eat/play）、去 e+ing（come/write/ride/dance）、**双写+ing（run/sit/cut/put）**。

**L21 一般现在时**：三用途——regularly（every day/morning/Monday、once a week）、all the time or sometimes（always/usually/often/sometimes）、always true（The earth goes around the sun. The sun rises in the east. One plus one is two.）；**主语=1（三单）→ verb+s**（The sheep eats. The fish swims.）；三单变化：+s（open/play/read/run）、+es（guess/mix/watch/wash/do）、y→ies（study/fly；**元音+y 只 +s：says/pays/plays/buys**）。

**L22 一般将来时**：两种——**will+do（predict/promise：It will rain tomorrow. I will help you.）vs be going to+do（decide/plan：I am going to buy a cake.）**；信号 tomorrow/this evening/soon/later/next week；缩写 I'll/You'll/He'll…；I'm/He's/We're going to…；否定 will not = won't；疑问 Will+主语…?

**L23 一般过去时**：happened in the past；信号 yesterday/just now/last year/this morning/in 2020/one year ago；be→was/were（否定 wasn't/weren't；疑问 Was/Were+主语）；动词过去式：+ed（open/play/jump/walk）、+d（close/like/live/move）、y→ied（study/try/marry）、不规则（**pay-paid、say-said**、am/is-was、are-were、have-had、eat-ate、do-did、go-went、read-read、write-wrote、sit-sat、see-saw、come-came、run-ran）；**否定 did not+原形 = didn't；疑问 Did+主语+原形**；现在 vs 过去对比句（I walk the dog every morning. / I walked the dog yesterday morning.）。
